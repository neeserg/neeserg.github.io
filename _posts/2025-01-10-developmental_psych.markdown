This is going to brief blog. Honestly I have not researched this as much as I would like to. But I came across this guy Ken Wilber and his integral theory. When I did further research into his idea I also came across spiral dynamics. Now this could be pseudoscience. But this blog is like brief interpetation/re-interpetation of those ideas. I think there might be some truth to it. 

The summary of the idea is this:
- Society and human beings go through stages as they develop further.
- This stages involve the interaction of individuals and the collective.
- Society is sort of a reflection of how its individuals are behaving developmentally.
- Progress goes by individual finding the societies dominant thought, breaking free from it and then replacing it with another thought.

Ill try and summarise the stages which I am talking about. This type of thinking can also be supported by memetics and evolutionary game theory. There is meme that is propogating because it has a dominant strategy. There is some mutation of the meme (an individual) that exploits the startegy with his own strategy, people start copying the new dominant startegy.

I will show this strategy as my own re-interpetation. The names remain the same but I might change the meaning.

### Stage 1 Beige (Individual based)
- Survival

This stage is the lowest stage. Reptilian brain stuff. Sex, hunger, instincts and fear. Most of us have been masters of this stage, but we can still succumb to it. This is all about survival. You want to survive and do whatever it takes. Life is the main driver and death the enemy.

This is also an individualistic stage. When in this stage you think about yourself and yourself only. And do whatever gets you to another day.

- Main motivation: Survival
- Main Contradiction: Death

### Stage 2 Purple Group Survival

- Group Survival


- Main motivation: Your genes get passed on
- Main contradiction: Competition






### Stage 3 Red Power

- Use Group survival for your gain, create hierarchy where you are at the top

- Main motivation: More resources
- Main contradiction: Convincing people to give up power




### Stage 4 Blue Power 

- The group uses hierarchy and gives it meaning and create stories. Stories have values that is notorised by god

- Main motivation: Power ordained by god. Order
- Main Contradiction: Fictional stories


### Stage 5 Orange

- You start to create your own stories. Your experience is what gives these stories values. 

- Main motivation: Pursuit of personal experience
- Main Contradiction: Competing interests


### Stage 5 Green

- The group recognises that every story and experience has equal value. Everyone's stories have equal weight. 

- Main motivation: Fairness
- Main contradiction: Relativism



### Stage 6 Yellow

- You are truth seeker, you want to find things that are in universal agreement

- Main motivation: Truth
- Main contradiction: Unknowability
